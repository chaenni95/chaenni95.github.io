Chaeyeon Yoo

Ph.D. Candidate · Department of Statistics, University of Connecticut

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at the University of Connecticut, advised by Professor Dipak K. Dey. My research focuses on developing principled Bayesian and machine learning methods for complex survival data, with particular interests in mixture cure rate models, variable selection, and high-dimensional data analysis.

⇩ Download CV ⇩ Research Statement ✉ Email ⎯ GitHub

Research Interests

Survival Analysis Bayesian Methodology Machine Learning High-Dimensional Data Analysis Variable Selection Mixture Cure Rate Models

My work lies at the intersection of statistical theory and modern biomedical data challenges. I develop novel Bayesian frameworks for survival outcomes with a cure fraction, with a focus on variable selection, flexible link functions, and scalable inference in high-dimensional settings.
